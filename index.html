Dav Lewis

Dav Lewis is a growing powerhouse of talent and heart. His ability to act, direct, and produce has made him a unique, sought-after figure in entertainment. He has appeared in films, television, and marketing campaigns, and you may recognize his latest work, 2nd Chances, an award-winning documentary inspired by real-life experiences. As an activist and entrepreneur, he is engaged in causes and programs that expand opportunities for underprivileged individuals across America.

His journey began in Terrell, Texas, where he was raised by his mother. He attended Abilene Christian University, where he was an All-American in football and track & field, and graduated with a degree in Biology. He then moved to Los Angeles to pursue entertainment, starting out in modeling, which led to a successful Adidas campaign. He later landed the lead role in the comedy series Not By Choice. His experience spans 15+ years, with most of his work behind the lens.

His passion for humanity led to WOKE WORLD, a clothing brand built to promote social change, and 2nd Chances.life, a nonprofit that supports people re-entering society after incarceration with counseling, job placement, housing, and education.

  • Director, Producer — 2nd Chances
  • Lead Actor — Not By Choice series
  • Lead Actor — Adidas Adizero campaign
  • Activist, Founder, CEO — Woke World, 2nd Chances.Life
